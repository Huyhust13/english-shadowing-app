The daily routine (20–30 minutes)

Small and daily beats long and rare. Twenty minutes every day for three months will change your English more than a weekend course. English Hub tracks three habits that train different skills:

1 · Vocab
Anki reviews
5–10 min

Remember words for the long term with spaced repetition.

2 · Practice
Write & say sentences
10 min

Use grammar and new words actively, and get corrected.

3 · Shadowing
Copy a native speaker
10 min

Train pronunciation, rhythm, stress and fluency.

A good order: do Vocab first (it's easy to start), then Practice using today's new words, then Shadowing when your voice is warmed up. Any order is fine; what matters is that all of them get done.

No time today? Do the minimum: clear your due Anki cards, write 3 sentences, and shadow one clip 3 times. Ten minutes keeps the habit (and the streak) alive.

1 · Vocab with Anki

Anki shows you each word just before you'd forget it, so a few minutes a day is enough to keep thousands of words.

Set up (once)

  1. Create a free account at ankiweb.net.
  2. On the Vocab page, log in with that account. Your decks sync here, to Anki desktop and to AnkiDroid (free on Android).
  3. No decks yet? New cards you add here go to My Words. You can also download a shared deck in Anki desktop (for example an IELTS or "4000 Essential English Words" deck).

Every day

  • Open Vocab → Study and clear the cards that are due. Be honest with the buttons: Again if you didn't know it. Shortcuts: Space to show the answer, 1–4 to answer.
  • Add 5–10 new words a day, not more. Too many new cards means a huge review pile next week.
  • Make good cards: the word, its meaning, one example sentence from where you met it, and the pronunciation (audio or IPA).
Best source of new words: your own Shadowing scripts and the corrections you get in Practice. Words you've met in context stick much better than words from a list.

2 · Sentence practice with an AI tutor

Reading and listening build understanding; producing sentences builds accuracy. Use any AI chat (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini…) as a patient tutor, then keep the useful parts on the Practice page.

How

  1. Open your AI chat and paste one of the prompts below.
  2. Write (or say, with voice input) your sentences. Try to use words from today's Anki cards.
  3. Copy the corrections that matter into your Practice note: your sentence, the corrected one, and why. Use highlight for the mistake and bold for the better phrase.
  4. Press Mark today's practice done. Come back later and press Save note if you add more.
Correct my sentences
You are my English tutor. My level is about IELTS 6.0 and I want to reach band 7. I will send you sentences. For each one: (1) give the corrected version, (2) explain each mistake in one short line, (3) give a more natural way a native speaker would say it. Keep it short. Then give me one similar sentence to translate or rewrite, so I practise the same point.
Speaking reflex drill
Let's do an IELTS speaking drill. Ask me one Part 1 question at a time on everyday topics (home, work, study, hobbies, food, travel). I'll answer in 2–4 sentences. After each answer: correct my mistakes briefly, suggest one better word or phrase, give me a band estimate, then ask the next question. After 5 questions, move to a Part 2 cue card.
Use today's words
Here are words I'm learning today: [paste 5–10 words]. For each word, give me one short example sentence and a common collocation. Then ask me to write my own sentence with each word, and correct them one by one.
Find my patterns (once a week)
Here are my practice notes from this week: [paste]. List my 3 most common mistake types (e.g. articles, tenses, prepositions), with one example of each, and give me a 5-minute exercise for the most important one.

3 · Shadowing

Shadowing means playing a short clip of a native speaker and speaking along with it, copying not just the words but the rhythm, stress, intonation and linking. It's the fastest way to sound natural.

Choose a clip

  • 1–3 minutes long, at your level (you understand about 80% without subtitles), with a transcript.
  • Good sources: BBC Learning English (6 Minute English), TED / TED-Ed talks, YouTube videos with good captions, podcasts with transcripts. Choose a voice you'd like to sound like.
  • Keep the same clip for 2–3 days. Repetition is the point.

Step by step (about 10 minutes)

  1. Listen twice without reading. Just get the meaning.
  2. Read the script while listening. Paste it into the notes on the Shadowing page. Highlight stressed words, mark linking (check‿it‿out) and pauses ( / ).
  3. Shadow with the script, 3–5 times. Speak at the same time as the speaker, or half a second behind. Slow the video to 0.75× at first if you need to.
  4. Shadow without the script, 2–3 times, at normal speed.
  5. Record yourself once (your phone's voice recorder is fine) and compare with the original. What sounds different?
  6. Log it: clip link (the title fills in automatically), number of reps, how it felt (Rough / OK / Smooth), and notes on what was hard.
Write down what was hard, not just "difficult": "stress on auTOnomous", "linking in a lot of", "too slow in the second sentence". That's what you'll fix next time, and how you'll see your progress.

Streak & reminders

  • The Today page shows what's left today and your streak: the number of days in a row with every habit done. Vocab only counts if you connected AnkiWeb.
  • Today not done yet doesn't break the streak: it counts from yesterday until midnight.
  • Set a daily reminder in Settings, by Telegram, by phone notification, or both. It only arrives on days you haven't finished, at your own time and in your own timezone.
  • Forgot to log yesterday? You can still edit past days: on Shadowing (Edit → change the date) and Practice (Edit in the history).
  • Missed a day? Start again. The habit matters more than the number.

Weekly review (15 minutes, e.g. Sunday)

  1. Read this week's Practice notes. Paste them into the "Find my patterns" prompt above.
  2. Shadow your favourite clip of the week once more, without the script. Notice how much easier it is.
  3. Add the 5 most useful phrases from your notes to Anki.
  4. Pick next week's clip.

On your phone

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Open English Hub from the new icon. Notifications only work in this home-screen app (iOS 16.4 or newer).
  • Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app.
  • Then turn on reminders in Settings and press Send a test reminder.

FAQ

Who can see my notes?

Only you. Each account is private; even the admin only sees names and when people last practised.

Is my AnkiWeb password stored?

No. Only a sync key is kept, like Anki desktop does. You can disconnect in Settings.

Forgot my password?

Ask the person who invited you for a reset link.

Can I keep a copy of my data?

Yes: Settings → Download my data.
